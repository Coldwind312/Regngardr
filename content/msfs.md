I spend quite a lot of time on *Microsoft Flight Simulator* 2024 (MSFS).

Some people are pretty disparaging about this particular sim, but honestly I love it. Graphically it's beautiful, it has some great planes and other cloudbotherers, and it just feels nice to fly, especially when you're in little light prop planes. Its closest rival, *X-Plane* 12, does look beautiful too, but what puts me off that one is a) the price (not unreasonable in isolation but I already own MSFS and I've less money to throw around than once I had), b) the range of planes in *X-Plane* is perfectly respectable too, but it lacks the Grumman Albatross and the Dornier Seastar, which are two firn favourites of mine. And c), *X-Plane* relies on purchased and installed scenery packs for areas you want to fly around. While MSFS streams scenery textures from Bing Maps. Now I should say I've never looked at *X-Plane* closely enough to see how much of an issue the scenery is outside of purchased areas, but I'm fairly confident I couldn't look down from moderately on high and see my exact house.
