Well, at the risk of looking all Internet 1.0, I thought I'd throw in a few factoids about myself, then offer you some of my Favourite Things (by means of which I sometimes Don't Feel So Bad—see if they do unto you likewise); and then, in a real retro move, some links. A proper, honest-to-gremlins blogroll. Because I'm old and it's my site and I can.

**THE DROW IN QUESTION**

My name is variable, for I am confused. Of the metric bagload of names I use for different things, the one most fitting for this place is Coldwind. And I am the keeper of this little Hollow in the Hedge.

![[coldwind-drow.jpg]]
*Not an entirely accurate rendition of me.*

I am a fifty-some-year-old trans woman with an identity problem. That plethora of different names and identities I mentioned isn't out of any desire to deceive or mislead, but rather because I've never really been able to figure out what my identity actually is. Sure, I have a state-registered name. I have documentation. But that only tells me what I'm *legally* called and sundry information about myself that the state keeps on file.

The state in this case is that known as the United Kingdom, or Britain. I live there but I'm not really *of* it, if you see what I mean. I don't feel any particular affinity with it (though it has some lovely scenery). It's just where I happened to show up and it's familiar.

I am a Roman. A *Roman pagan*, I would clarify for the benefit of those in whose eyes I'm concerned about looking minimally nuts—but in reality it's a little more tangly than that.

I am a pagan, in the loosest possible sense. The extent to which I'm actually 'religious' at all is something I have yet to figure out. When I think of the idea of divinity it's the gods of Rome that I see. But the truth is I don't do much with them. The actual Romans would've called me impious, even irreligious—but the truth is I just don't think the gods care all that much what I do or say. Religion is something I intend to go into in some of the pages here at some stage.

Ancient Rome itself, though, is... Well, let's call it a *special interest*. I have long held an all-consuming fascination with the place, the time, the people, the language—with a couple of particular specifications. First is that I'm not very fond of the Romans. They were pretty godsawful people for the most part. At least, their rich and powerful 'movers and shakers' were almost to a man entirely vile. The common people I imagine were basically just like common people everywhere: just trying to get their lives lived without being too badly moved and shook.

Also, when I talk of my fascination with and unaccountable sense of connection to ancient Rome, know that I'm a republican through and through (the small-r Roman sort, not the Nazi American sort). As far as I'm concerned the early Roman Kingdom was just the larval stage of the Republic. And the Empire (as we recognise it today), as powerful as it might have become, was the failure state. The death of democracy and the collapse back into monarchy.

Like I said: tangly.

I am Drow. A *dark elf*, or *niðalfr*. Not literally, perhaps (the images you might see of drow round here are mostly, in full or in part, from *Baldur's Gate III*), but in psychological and philosophical and emotional terms, most definitely. I am a creature of much fury, which I'm not proud of. But I do at the very least try to point in the proper directions. Against fascism, for example. I tend towards the deeply cynical and the uninclined to assume good faith. It's a product of a long time spent working in an industry that doesn't reward generosity of spirit, optimism and trust, and whose shadow I suspect will always hang over me, even though I don't work in it any more.

I'm a Squall. A being of water and air. I love rivers, lakes and the sea. Waterfalls and rain, and the wind in trees and blustery days and roiling clouds and storms and lashing waves and gently rippling waters. I'm entranced by deep pools and lochs, the eerie hadal oceans, and the light playing on the sandy bed of a shallow stream on a warm day.

Whether I'm Scottish or not depends who you ask. The approximate human form with me in it lives in the Midlands of England. But it is half Scottish by heritage, and has—perhaps because of a well-known phenomenon relating to grass colouration—always felt a rather stronger affinity with that country than with the one in which it actually lives.

I don't like to think of myself as a gamer, but if I must—by dint of the fact that I've wasted so much of my precious time on this Earth playing video games—then I would like to stress that I am a gamer, and not a Gamer™. Some of the games I enjoy most are listed in my Faves page.
