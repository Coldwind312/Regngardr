In the corner there's a desk and a chair. On the desk is all the paraphernalia of the writer's art: pens, pencils, an Amstrad PCW with a classic green screen...  It's mostly brand-new (apart from the PCW) and all relatively untouched. The owner is clearly someone who has aspirations to authorship but, perhaps, doesn't have the focus or the commitment to actually do the work. Pinned to the wall just above the desk is a small plaque that reads "I'm With Butler".

They've obviously managed one or two bits of writing here and there, though. You riffle through the papers.
