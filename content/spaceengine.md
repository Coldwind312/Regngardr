[[space]][[space]]*[SpaceEngine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/314650/SpaceEngine/)* by Cosmographic Software is a '3D planetarium', a computer program that models... everything. The whole universe. More or less, albeit only to a given level of detail.

But the level of detail is pretty damned impressive.

Start on Earth. Orbit a bit. Enjoy. It's very pretty. Go to the Moon, Take in the detailed surface mapping and have a look at craters. Lots of craters. And maria. That's 'seas'; the plural of 'mare' in Latin. Not Maria, like, anybody you know called Maria.

Then hop around the planets in the solar system, check out their moons. Bounce off a couple of asteroids and thumb your nose at a passing comet or two. Then it's off into the Outer Darkness looking for pretty stars and peculiar orbital configurations and even the odd black hole. Nebulas? Oh my, yes. Such nebulas. And if you zoom out far enough you can leave the Galaxy behind and find a universe brimming with, well, lots more of them.

Lots.
And.
Lots.

*SpaceEngine* uses astronomical data for bodies we know about and models those with some pretty high fidelity. Where the patchy gaps start happening where we don't have solid data, SpaceEngine uses procedural generation to fill in the gaps. Though at all times generated bodies are clearly marked and can be filtered out if you're all about the facts and only the facts.

Here on this page I'll probably, at some point, put a few of my wanderings on *SpaceEngine* as I find interesting things.
