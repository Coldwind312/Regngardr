You make your way down the path from the main clearing towards what was advertised to you as a harbour. What you find... *sort* of qualifies.

There is a sea here. Not entirely unexpected of a harbour, you might think. The sea is clear, and there's a tang of salt water in the air.

There may or may not be rain—relaxing rain, that is; gentle but evocative. Or perhaps it's a warm sunny day, a cool blue sky dotted with gleaming cumulus clouds; the leaves of the forest glittering emerald green, and the waves lapping gently, sparkling in the sunlight. Or maybe it's night-time. In which case, the harbour's lit mostly by torches arrayed around the walls and along the path.

The walls extend out and round, making for a sheltered bay in which lies, somewhat counterintuitively, an aeroplane. A large flying boat, high-winged, with two big propeller engines. It's painted in shades of grey, and under the cockpit side windows is stencilled the name *Heavy Rain*.

This plane—which due to the strange magics hereabouts looks oddly small for such a large aircraft—is a Grumman G-111 Albatross. It's old, but spacious and versatile. And its owner occasionally uses it to fly around looking at places of mystery, myth and folklore.
