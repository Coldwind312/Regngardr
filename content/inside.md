Inside, things are pretty much like you'd expect having seen the outside. It's a comfortable space, warm, lit by either oil lamps and fairy lights, or by daylight if applicable.

![[leanto-int-03.webp]]

There's actually more room than you might have expected, though. It's spacious enough for a small group to gather together as long as people don't mind sitting on chests and the bed and a rough worktop at the back.

You can tell a lot [[aboutme|about a person]] by the state of their ramshackle hut.
