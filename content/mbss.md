Please forgive the *uncouth* title. But space is, after all, pretty mind-bending, and honestly sometimes the vocabulary kind of gives out in the face of it and just goes... *That... is some mind-bending shit right there.*

I'm going to tell you about some of my very favourite of that shit, and I want you to keep in mind that I am not, nor do I claim to be, any kind of scientist with any kind of qualifications. I am a very enthusiastic amateur who's going to do her best to present this stuff as accurately as layman's terms allow. But, if something here piques your interest, I would consider it a job well done on my part if you'd say, "That sounds fascinating. I think I'll go off and look up more about it from someone who actually knows what they're talking about."

Five. Four. Three. Two. One.

Liftoff.
