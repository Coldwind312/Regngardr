A blog roll. A real, live, eating and breathing blog roll. It is like Jurassic Park up in here.

Hey, don’t knock it, though. There’s some useful stuff out there in Internet-land.

There's the horrors, obviously, but we're trying to keep those to a minimum here, and we're concentrating instead on things like:

---
### **Aviation and Flight Simulation**

- [E6BX](https://e6bx.com/) : online aviation calculator, offering METAR/TAF weather reports, weight and balance calculations, pressure and density altitude, wind components, a holding pattern calculator and more.

---
### **Astronomy and Space**

- [The European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/): the joint space agency of Europe. But you knew that. They’re one of several space agencies that conspiracy theorists don’t seem to realise exist; or at least will tell you are basically just branches of NASA designed to lull us SHEEPLE or whatever godsdamned nonsense.
- [The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)](https://www.nasa.gov/) : the space agency of the United States of America. Offers scientific information about spaceflight and space research, details and logs of missions manned, automated and static, and prevents the SHEEPLE from reaching the Ice Wall around the Edge of the Flat Earth[1](https://squalllines.wordpress.com/?page_id=10919&preview=true#ec1ea7ef-ac39-4f59-ac75-b37134997e38).

---
### **Words and That**

- [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) : the English edition of a comprehensive user-maintained dictionary, offering etymologies, derivations, synonyms, usage notes, quotations and, for those languages where it’s relevant, declension and conjugation tables.
- [Online Etymology Dictionary](https://etymonline.com/) : the legendary ‘Etymonline’. An impressive dictionary mapping ‘wheel-ruts of modern English’. Trusted and reliable, the site offers dives into English words and explains how they evolved and where and when usages emerged.
- [RhymeZone](https://rhymezone.com/) : excellent site for searching not only for rhymes but also synonyms, homophones, phrase rhymes and more.

---
### **Writing and Story**

- [Fantasy Name Generators](https://fantasynamegenerators.com/) : a huge—fully _huge_—selection of random and algorithmic name generators, producing names patterned after numerous real-world cultures, fantasy and science-fiction character types, and established pop-culture franchises. Also offers an assortment of other generators for places, objects, ships, magic spells, creatures—the list goes on.

---
### **Games**

- [Fallen London](https://www.fallenlondon.com/) : free browser game set in Fallen London—a once-prominent British city stolen by bats and taken deep underground; now located on the shores of a giant subterranean lake, a sunless sea. Browser-based, largely consisting of beautiful artwork and the most _amazingly_ imaginative writing.

---
### **General Reference**

- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/) : comprehensive user-written and -maintained encyclopaedia. Actually pretty well self-regulating, but generally includes solid referencing to check any statements made.
- [Behind The Name](https://www.behindthename.com/) : charting the etymology and usage patterns of first names in numerous cultures and languages.
- [Behind The Surname](https://surnames.behindthename.com/) : companion site to the above, offering the same details about surnames.

---
### **Politics and Media**

- [Media Bias Fact Check](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/) : search for news sources—newspapers, websites, TV, radio and online broadcasting—and check out assessments of their political lean (or outright bias) and the factuality of their reporting.

---
### **Tools**

- [Proton](https://proton.me/) : privacy-focused email, VPN and cloud storage services. Free tier available, paid plans offer bigger faster stronger.
