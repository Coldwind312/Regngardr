You follow the path up a gentle incline until it levels out and the trees give way to open, grassy plains. In the distance you see a range of mountains, snow-capped. From the summit of the highest extend white feathers of snow, blown by the winds across the clear blue sky.

Below the mountains on the wide, even grassland you see a town. It's not a huge place, but it's clearly bustling. Clean, silvery spires and rounded structures give a futuristic impression, boosted even further by the sight of spacecraft arriving and departing from landing pads outside the settlement. As ships climb out of sight into the bright blue, you can hear the faint crackle of rocket 

The footpath you're following comes to an end at a small structure that appears somewhere between a bus stop and a train station. A maglev line runs from the station away toward the town, and from there you can take a shuttle to... well, *everywhere*.
