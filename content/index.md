The long and winding path has led you up the hillside and into the woods. The earth is soft under your feet, with the occasional half-buried root snaking across the way, and twigs here and there that snap at your tread. Hobbits would be tutting.

![[on-the-path.webp]]

But there's no need for stealth here. This isn't a place of danger. Despite the deepening shadows under the further trees, despite the greening of the light as you follow the trail up to the top of the last ridge, there's a sense of welcome here.

Now you step down into a wide clearing.

![[in-the-clearing.webp]]

In the centre of the clearing a well-maintained fire pit smokes gently, embers glowing. A cauldron of dull copper hangs from a tripod stand over the fire. It's not magic. It's thoroughly culinary. There are low benches made of half-logs set around, and positioned outside them, a couple of tables are strewn with bottles and plates.

At the far edge of the clearing there's an approximate building. Well, more a sort of stacking. Hewn planks, some more weather-beaten than others, lashed together with indeterminate twine. It has a roof, high across the front, sloping down towards the back. From each opposing gable, a wooden raven stands gazes out into the surrounding forest. The front eave tops a wall that's really not doing much other than keeping the windows off the ground. The windows themselves are little more than places where there isn't wood, and the door is squat to the point of being almost square.

A trio of paths, one to the left of the barely-shack, the others to the right, lead away into the further woods. Each has a sign: to the left, the sign says, "To the Harbour". The sign on the right nearest to you reads, "To Space!". And squinting at the last, on the path setting out from further back around the clearing edge, you see it just says, "The Deep".
